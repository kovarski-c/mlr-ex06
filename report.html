Preparation is limited to the exercise's two dimensions - numeric mean/median imputation and original versus engineered features (available_wh, wh_per_km, computed after fold-fitted imputation). The fixed physical cleaning (charge outside [0,100], distance ≤ 0 → NaN) and the categorical "missing" label + one-hot encoding run in every pipeline unchanged. StandardScaler is applied only to k-NN, SVM and logistic regression, after numeric feature creation; tree models get no scaler. Imputation and scaling are fitted inside each CV fold.

Limitations and a cautious conclusion

  • Selection optimism. The winner is the maximum of 100 noisy 3-fold CV estimates; its CV mean is an optimistic value, not an unbiased performance estimate. Only the single test evaluation after fixing all choices is (weakly) honest - and it is itself one draw on 450 trips (±~1.5 percentage points, roughly).
  • Fold SD is context, not a significance test. Differences smaller than the fold SDs should not be read as "better model".
  • Accuracy on a 61/39 task. The majority-class classifier already reaches 61.3% CV accuracy; accuracy hides per-class behaviour and was kept as the only metric to stay comparable with the notebook.
  • Search budget. 20 TPE trials per family is a teaching budget; rankings could shift with more trials, more folds or another seed.
  • Synthetic teaching data. These are simulated trips with injected missing values and impossible readings, not measurements. Nothing here is a validated battery model and this must not be presented as a real battery-safety or fire-risk predictor.

Conclusion: gradient-boosted and random-forest trees reach the best CV accuracy; linear and distance-based models trail them by a small margin that is comparable to the fold SD. The engineered features (available_wh, wh_per_km) appear in the better configurations, consistent with the notebook's feature experiment, but the margin is within the noise of this budget. The result reproduces the notebook's pipeline (baseline agreement above) and the test subset was used exactly once, after every choice was fixed.
